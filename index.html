a letter

Just for You

Dear Mumma,

🌸 ✦ 🌸

You're not just my mother. You're my first home, my greatest teacher, my biggest comfort, and the person I look up to more than anyone else in the world. You've always been the strongest, kindest, smartest person I know. Honestly, you're still the best physics teacher I've ever had, and somehow the only person who could explain both life and science in ways that actually made sense.

🤍 ✦ 🤍

One of my favourite things growing up was watching you and Papa together. The way he looks at you, the way you both care for each other, laugh together, stand by each other through everything taught me what love really looks like long before I ever understood it. The home the two of you built wasn't just full of happiness. It was full of safety, warmth, and the quiet certainty that no matter what happened, everything would be okay because you were there.

🌸 ✦ 🌸

I don't think I've ever properly told you just how loved you've made me feel throughout my life. I have never once doubted that I mattered. I have never wondered whether someone was in my corner. That's because of you. Every sacrifice you made, every late night, every small thing you did without expecting thanks, every conversation, every hug, every word of encouragement all became the foundation of the person I am today.

🤍 ✦ 🤍

Living away from home has made me appreciate all of this even more. For the first time, I truly understand how much you did every single day without ever making it seem difficult. The older I get, the more I realise that so many of the things I admire in people are things I've simply been lucky enough to grow up seeing in you. Your patience, your strength, your curiosity, your kindness, your resilience, and your endless ability to put the people you love before yourself are the qualities I hope I carry with me for the rest of my life.

🌸 ✦ 🌸

Whenever I'm unsure of what to do, I find myself thinking, "What would Mumma do?" You have always been my example of what it means to be a genuinely good person. If I become even half the person you are, I'll consider that one of the greatest achievements of my life.

🤍 ✦ 🤍

I know I'm far away this year, and that's probably been the hardest part of starting this new chapter. It's not the work or the new city. It's simply not being a room away from you anymore. But no amount of distance could ever change what you've given me. Home has never just been a place to me. Home has always been you.

🌸 ✦ 🌸

So I built you this instead of a card. It's small, but every part of it is real. It's a little collection of our memories, our years together, and all the love and gratitude I sometimes struggle to put into words. I wanted to make something that reminds you, even when I'm far away, just how much you mean to me.

💗

Happy Birthday, Mumma.

🤍 ✦ 🤍

Thank you for giving me a childhood filled with love, a life filled with confidence, and a heart that knows what unconditional love feels like. Thank you for believing in me before I believed in myself, for celebrating every little win, for picking me up after every setback, and for always making me feel like I could come home, no matter what.

🌸 ✦ 🌸

I hope this year brings you even a fraction of the happiness you've spent your whole life giving everyone around you. You deserve every good thing this world has to offer.

I love you more than any words, any distance, or any lifetime could ever measure.

With all my love,
Your favourite (and only) child ❤️
